Expected months per year for which the 12 month Standardized Precipitation Evapotranspiration Index (SPEI) is below a threshold.

$$
I = \sum_{m = 1}^{12} \mathbb{1}_{\; \, S_m < s^\text{thresh}}, \qquad i = \mathbb{E}(I) \nonumber
$$

$i$ is the indicator and $I$ the number of months below the threshold in a single year, $S_m$ is the 12 month SPEI for the month with index $m$ and $s^\text{thresh}$ is the threshold index.

The expectation is estimated by averaging over a 20-year window.
