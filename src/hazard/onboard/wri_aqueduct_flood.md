Riverine and coastal flood depth (in metres), unprotected -- i.e. with no allowance for flood defences -- at nine return periods: 1-in-2, 1-in-5, 1-in-10, 1-in-25, 1-in-50, 1-in-100, 1-in-250, 1-in-500 and 1-in-1000 years. Coverage is global, at a spatial resolution of 30 × 30 arc seconds (approx. 1 km at the equator). Riverine sets are available for a historical baseline (1980) and, for five GCMs, for RCP 4.5 and RCP 8.5 in 2030, 2050 and 2080; coastal sets are available with and without subsidence, at the 5th, 50th and 95th percentiles of sea level rise.

Produced by the [World Resources Institute (WRI) Aqueduct Floods](https://www.wri.org/aqueduct) project, building on the Global Flood Risk with IMAGE Scenarios (GLOFRIS) model. Source: Ward, P. J., et al. (2020). Aqueduct Floods Methodology. Technical Note. Washington, D.C.: World Resources Institute. <https://www.wri.org/research/aqueduct-floods-methodology>. Because flood defences are not represented, the indicator will tend to overstate depth and extent where defences exist.
