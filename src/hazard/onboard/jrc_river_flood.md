River flood depth (in metres), unprotected -- i.e. with no allowance for flood defences -- at six return periods: 1-in-10, 1-in-20, 1-in-50, 1-in-100, 1-in-200 and 1-in-500 years. Coverage spans most of continental Europe together with the river basins draining into the Mediterranean and Black Seas across the Caucasus, Middle East and Northern Africa, at 100m resolution.

Produced by the European Commission's [Joint Research Centre (JRC)](https://data.jrc.ec.europa.eu/dataset/1d128b6c-a4ee-4858-9e34-6210707f3c81). Source: Dottori, F., Alfieri, L., Bianchi, A., Skoien, J., and Salamon, P. (2022). A new dataset of river flood hazard maps for Europe and the Mediterranean Basin. *Earth System Science Data*, 14, 1549–1569. <https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-14-1549-2022>. Depths are simulated river discharges (LISFLOOD) routed onto the terrain with a hydrodynamic model (LISFLOOD-FP), without representing flood defences, so the indicator will tend to overstate depth and extent where defences exist. Dottori et al. (2022) compare the resulting maps against official, defended flood hazard maps for Hungary, Italy (Po basin), Norway, Spain and England: at the 1-in-100-year return period they capture, on average, around two-thirds of the reference flood extent, with the largest overstatement at return periods below 100 years.
